I adds up error over time. On a gravity arm it is rarely the right first move. Gravity changes with angle, so I would have to relearn the answer at every angle, slowly. Worse is windup. Here something holds the arm at the stop for a second and a half while the total keeps growing. When it lets go, the arm has to overshoot to bring the total back down. WPILib limits the I term to plus or minus 1 by default, the blue line. We widened it to 12 with setIntegratorRange for the orange one. There is a better fix for sag, coming next.
