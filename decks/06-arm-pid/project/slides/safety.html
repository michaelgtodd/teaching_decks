Best practice

Safety goes in before tuning

Add soft limits, a current limit and an output clamp first. Keep people clear and a hand on the disable key.

Start with low gains. On the Driver Station, Enter disables and Space is the emergency stop.

// NEO rotations, because the NEO's encoder is the selected sensor.

config.softLimit

    .forwardSoftLimit(armRadToMotorRotations(SOFT_MAX_RAD))

    .forwardSoftLimitEnabled(true)

    .reverseSoftLimit(armRadToMotorRotations(SOFT_MIN_RAD))

    .reverseSoftLimitEnabled(true);

Safety is not optional. Before tuning, add soft limits, a current limit and an output clamp. REV suggests 40 to 60 amps for a NEO, and the default is 80. One person keeps a hand on the disable key, and everyone else stands clear.
