Deck 6 · Arm PID

2

Three goals. First, explain: if you can describe what each term does to an arm, you understand it. Second, tune: there is a safe order, and following it saves hardware. Third, build: we write real command-based Java, first on the roboRIO where you can see every number, then on the motor controller. Mentors, the two exercises need a laptop per pair, and the second needs the deck 6 code loaded.
