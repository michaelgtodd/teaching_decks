Why 0° is flat

Try this: hold a backpack with your arm straight out, then raise it straight up. Straight out is the hard part, because gravity pulls down and the weight is far from your shoulder. Straight up, the weight sits right over the joint. That reach is the cosine of the angle. We found these holding volts by searching the motor model at each angle, and they trace a cosine curve exactly. That is why flat is the sensible zero: cos of zero is one, so kG is one easy thing to measure, and WPILib expects it.
