4

Three terms. The setpoint is where you want the arm. The measurement is where the encoder says it is. The error is the gap between them. The controller turns error into volts, the arm moves, the encoder reads it, and around we go, every 20 milliseconds. All the math goes in the controller box. The rest of the theory is what to put in there.
