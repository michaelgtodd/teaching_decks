Deck 6 · Arm PID

9

Nothing is free. D looks at the change between two readings and divides by the loop time, which is 0.02 seconds. Dividing by 0.02 is multiplying by 50, so a tiny flicker in the encoder becomes a jump in volts. Here the arm is holding still, and we added fake noise to the reading. The volts spread 0.33 volts without D and 0.91 with it. Too much D and the motor buzzes or heats up. If you are short on time, this slide can be one sentence.
