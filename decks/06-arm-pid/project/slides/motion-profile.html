A motion profile moves the setpoint smoothly, so the arm gets a sequence of small, reachable goals. Plot speed against time and you get a trapezoid: up, flat, down. WPILib’s TrapezoidProfile does this with a top speed and an acceleration, here 2.5 rad/s and 6 rad/s squared. The jump pulls 105 amps in this model, which has no current limit. A real SPARK would clip that. Now the profile gives kV a target speed, and feedforward does 88 percent of the work.
