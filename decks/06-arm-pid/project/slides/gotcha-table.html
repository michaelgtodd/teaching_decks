Read these by symptom, because that is how you will meet them. Units: 45 means 45 radians, and our clamp sends the arm to the soft limit. Range: the goal is past a stop, so the motor pushes forever. Ratio: the NEO’s encoder and soft limits depend on it. Homing: a relative encoder reads zero wherever it wakes up. Sign: our arm with kG backwards sagged 3.5 degrees, twice the 1.7 from no kG.
