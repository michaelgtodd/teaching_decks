D watches how fast the error is changing. Closing in fast? It pushes back, before you arrive. Think of driving to a stop sign: you do not wait until you pass the line to brake. Overshoot falls from 19.6 degrees to 0.5. But look at the end of the blue line. It still stops short, because once the arm stops moving, D adds nothing. Hold that thought. Using the same kP makes this a fair test, so only D changed.
