Only a few lines change. PIDController becomes ProfiledPIDController, which owns a TrapezoidProfile. calculate moves the setpoint one step and runs PID against it. getSetpoint gives the target speed, which goes to kV. Call reset with the real angle when each move starts, or the profile starts from wherever it last stopped. Expect this move, from -30 to 45 degrees, to stay within 2 degrees of the goal after 0.78 seconds, peaking at 4.8 volts. That is under the 6 volt clamp.
