This is a simulated arm in Java with the same numbers as our slides, so what you see in sim should look like the plots. SingleJointedArmSim takes the motor, gearing, inertia, length, angle limits, a gravity switch and a starting angle. Mechanism2d is the stick drawing. Each loop, update passes the simulated SPARK’s applied volts to the physics, then gives the angle back to the simulated encoder. To watch it, open the Mechanism tab in AdvantageScope and drag in the Arm entry, or use the Mechanism2d widget in the sim GUI.
