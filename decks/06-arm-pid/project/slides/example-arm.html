Meet our arm. The motor numbers are the real NEO constants from WPILib’s DCMotor class. The bar is uniform, the same shape WPILib’s SingleJointedArmSim assumes, so your Java sim later matches these slides. We simulate the motor, the gearbox, the inertia and gravity, and we step it every 20 milliseconds like the robot. The encoder reading is one loop old because a SPARK sends it over CAN every 20 milliseconds by default. Real arms add friction, and this one has none.
