The rule

Why it stays

Holding there takes 0.95 V. P only makes volts from error, so some error has to stay.

P is the simplest idea in control: the farther away you are, the harder you push. Now watch what gravity does. The arm stops short and stays short. To hold still it needs 0.95 volts, and P can only make volts from error. So it settles where error times kP equals what gravity needs. Check it: 6.8 degrees is 0.118 radians, times 8 is 0.95 volts.
