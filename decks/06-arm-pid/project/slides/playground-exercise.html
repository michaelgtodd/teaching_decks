Exercise

Test gains in the arm playground

1.

Scan the Arm playground QR on the last slide, or open docs/arm-playground/index.html from the repo.

2.

Press P only. Write down how far short the arm stops.

3.

Press Too much P, then slide kP down until the shaking stops.

4.

Find your own gains. Turn on the profile if you want it.

Done when: the status line reads Settled and you can read your gains as Java constants.
