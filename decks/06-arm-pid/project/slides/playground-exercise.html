This page is a simulation of our same arm, with the same physics, running on your own laptop or phone. It needs no internet and no install, and it opens straight from the file. You cannot hurt anything, so try silly numbers. Set kG negative. Set kP to 80. The status line tells you what happened, and Settled means within 1 degree, under 2 degrees of overshoot, and not shaking. Timing: 5 minutes, ending around minute 30. Ask one pair to read out their gains.
