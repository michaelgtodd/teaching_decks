Common mistake

The arm runs away at full speed

What you see

You enable, and the arm slams to a stop as fast as it can go.

Why it happens

Motor and encoder disagree on direction. Moving “toward” the goal makes the error bigger, so P pushes harder.

The fix

Robot disabled: lift the arm by hand. The angle must go up. Then at about 1 V: the arm must rise. Flip ENCODER_INVERTED or MOTOR_INVERTED.

Deck 6 · Arm PID

29

This is the dangerous one. Feedback only works if pushing up makes the reading go up. If they disagree, the correction makes the error worse, and it keeps getting worse until the arm hits something. Check both directions before you enable a closed loop. Hands on the arm, robot disabled, for the encoder. Then a very low voltage, with the arm clear, for the motor. In REVLib both are config settings: inverted on the motor config and inverted on absoluteEncoder.
