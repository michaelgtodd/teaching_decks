1

Native units

p is duty cycle per NEO rotation. kP 30 is 0.26. Start low.

2

kCos, not kG

WPILib’s kG is REV’s kCos. REV’s kG is for elevators.

3

kCosRatio

NEO rotations to arm rotations: 1 / 60. The sensor must read 0 at flat.

4

Send it once

Goals are in NEO rotations. MAXMotion uses kMAXMotionPositionControl.

30

REV multiplies kCos by the cosine of the arm position, and position times kCosRatio must give arm rotations. Flat must read zero, and a through-bore without zero centering wraps just below flat. So this loop uses the NEO’s encoder, with the through-bore’s angle copied in. That matches REV’s advice to home from an absolute encoder. Watch the names: WPILib kG means REV kCos. kCos applies in position modes. Gains change units: error is in NEO rotations and output is duty cycle, so 30 volts per radian becomes 30 over 12, times 2 pi over 60. D units differ too, so retune it from low.
