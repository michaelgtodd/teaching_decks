So turn up P. The sag shrinks, and a new problem shows up. The arm is moving fast when it reaches the setpoint, and P only starts pushing back after it has gone past. Our reading is also a little late. At kP 50 each swing feeds the next one, and it never settles. On a real arm that is broken chain and scared mentors. This is the trade: more P means less sag and more shake. We need something that looks at speed.
