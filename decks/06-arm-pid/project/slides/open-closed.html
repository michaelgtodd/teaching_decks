Control theory

Open loop and closed loop

Open loop: set it and walk away

You turn the hot tap to the spot that worked yesterday.

Nobody checks the water.

If someone flushes a toilet, you get a surprise.

Closed loop: keep checking

You turn the tap, then feel the water.

Too cold? Turn it more. Too hot? Turn it back.

Your hand is the sensor. That check is called feedback.

Start with a shower. Open loop means you pick an output and hope. It works until something changes. Closed loop means you measure the result and correct it, again and again. Your hand is the sensor and your brain is the controller. A thermostat does the same thing with a room. Ask the room: which is a robot arm more like?
