Control theory

Tuning steps, in order

1

kG first

All gains at 0. Raise kG until the arm stays where you leave it.

2

kS, only if needed

If the arm sticks and then jumps, add a small kS.

3

Raise kP

Start low and raise it in steps. Stop when it starts to overshoot.

4

Add kD

Add D until the overshoot goes. If the motor buzzes, back off.

5

Test with small, safe moves

Try 10° before 90°. Hand on the disable key, every time.

Deck 6 · Arm PID

18

Feedforward first, because it is the part you can get right without any oscillation. To measure kG and kS on a real arm, REV’s guide says: find V1, the most volts that do not lift it, and V2, the fewest that do not drop it. Then kS is V1 minus V2, over 2, and kG is V2 plus kS. Do not leave an arm hanging on motor power for long while you do this, because it heats up. Only then touch kP and kD.
