Exercise

Run the timed autonomous

1.

Put the robot on blocks, wheels off the ground. Deploy.

2.

In Driver Station, choose Autonomous and enable. Watch the wheels.

3.

Set PAUSE_SECONDS to 2.0, deploy, and run it again.

Done when: the wheels drive forward, stop for two seconds, then turn, and the terminal prints Auto finished.

24

One person stays ready on Enter to disable, and Space is the emergency stop. On blocks, “turn” means the left and right wheels spin opposite ways. The routine ends by itself after its last step. Then the default command, the gamepad drive, takes over again, so keep hands off the sticks.
