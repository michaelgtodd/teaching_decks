(1.5 min) These curves are exact math, the same formula as MathUtil.copyDirectionPow. Input shaping means changing the stick’s meaning without changing its range. Most driving happens at small movements, and that is where a curve spreads your fingers over more of the stick. It is like a car’s accelerator: the first bit of travel is gentle. Here is a surprise: because our scale happens before arcadeDrive squares the value, 0.7 turns into 0.49 at full stick. FIRST’s KitBot code does the same thing, and it is fine for a first drive. But now you know where your top speed went.
