Common mistake

CIMs set to kBrushless

What you see

The SPARK light shows magenta or cyan, the brushless colours, and may blink orange and magenta for a sensor fault.

Why it happens

A new SPARK ships set to brushless and can’t detect the motor. In that mode it looks for a motor sensor a CIM doesn’t have.

The fix

Use MotorType.kBrushed in code, and set the motor type to brushed in REV Hardware Client 2. Look for blue or yellow.

Blue or yellow means brushed mode. Cyan or magenta means brushless mode. Orange and magenta blinking is REV’s sensor fault, and REV says to expect it with a brushed motor until you set the type. REV warns that the wrong type can damage the SPARK, the motor or both. Never run a NEO in brushed mode: REV says that can permanently damage it.
