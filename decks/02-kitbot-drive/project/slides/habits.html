Constants: at an event, when a SPARK dies and the spare has another ID, you change one line. Ownership: if motors are private, no command can bypass the subsystem and its requirements. Factories: FIRST ships both styles, and the guide lists less boilerplate as a strength of inline commands. Classes also work. Names: when the scheduler reports a slow loop, it prints each command by name. Commit: small commits make it easy to return to working code.
