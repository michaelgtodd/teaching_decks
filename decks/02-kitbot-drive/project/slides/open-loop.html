Control theory is the study of making a system do what you asked. This robot is open loop: the code picks a voltage and never measures what happened. Closed loop measures the result and adjusts. Battery level, carpet, weight and friction all change the real speed, and open-loop code cannot detect that. With a human driver, the driver supplies the feedback. In autonomous drive by time, nothing corrects the error. Deck 8 shows how motors behave, and Deck 9 shows how to make open-loop driving feel better.
