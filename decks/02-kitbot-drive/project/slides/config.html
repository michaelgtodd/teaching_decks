A config is a list of settings, and apply copies one into another. All four SPARKs share the current limit and brake mode. Voltage compensation goes on the leaders only. A follower mirrors its leader’s output, so it gets the compensated voltage anyway. Deck 8 explains what voltage compensation and the current limit do. The two sides are mirror images, so one must be inverted. FIRST inverts the left, but your gearboxes decide: if forward goes backward, invert the other side. Followers pick up the inversion from their leader, and calling inverted on a follower has no effect.
