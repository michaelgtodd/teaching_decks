(1.5 min) The KitBot has six motors, and four of them are on the drivetrain, two CIMs per side. Each CIM has its own SPARK MAX over CAN. The IDs come from Constants, not typed numbers. Notice MotorType.kBrushed on all four. It is an easy one to get wrong, so we will look at what that looks like. DifferentialDrive turns “go forward and turn” into a left and a right output. It only needs the two leaders.
