Best practice

Never make the loop wait

No while loops or Thread.sleep in robot code.

Everything shares one 20 ms loop. If one command waits, every subsystem waits, and motor safety stops the drive.

// Wrong: the whole robot freezes for 2 seconds

Thread.sleep(2000);

 

// Right: the scheduler keeps looping while this runs

drive.driveArcade(() -> 0.5, () -> 0).withTimeout(2.0);

(1 min) Imagine the head chef stops to watch one pot for two minutes. Nothing else in the kitchen happens. The same goes for your code: while a loop waits, no buttons are read and no motors are updated. You will see “Loop time of 0.02s overrun” and “CommandScheduler loop overrun”. After 0.1 seconds without an update, motor safety stops the drive and reports “DifferentialDrive... Output not updated often enough.” That safety feature is on your side. So describe what should happen over time, with decorators like withTimeout, and let the scheduler do the waiting.
