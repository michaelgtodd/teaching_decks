Deck 8 · Understanding motors

7

(1.5 min) Same four numbers, four motors. Ask: which is strongest? Kraken, at 7.09 newton metres. Which uses the least current for its torque? NEO, at 0.025 newton metres per amp against 0.018 for a CIM. Stall current is a warning, not a target. Peak power sits at half speed, so it takes about half of stall current: 68 amps for a CIM and 184 for a Kraken. A 40 amp breaker won’t let you stay there. REV quotes 406 watts for the NEO, and ours is the straight-line estimate. FOC, in Deck 7, is a second set of Kraken numbers.
