Deck 8 · Understanding motors

10

Computed: each battery is a voltage behind a resistance, the same idea as WPILib’s BatterySim, with wiring included. The two batteries are assumptions, the same two as Deck 3. A battery is not a perfect 12 volts: pull more current and the voltage drops. The worn one hits the line at about 171 amps, the fresh one at about 300. Our launch number assumes four CIMs and no slip. A brownout is the roboRIO protecting itself: motors stop, then return above 7.5 volts. Breakers protect wires and watch heat over time, so they can’t stop a sag. The next slides can.
