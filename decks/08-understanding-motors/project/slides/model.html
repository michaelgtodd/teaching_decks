Deck 8 · Understanding motors

(2 min) Here is the whole deck on one slide. Voltage is how fast the motor wants to spin. Current is how hard it is pushing. Note the second box: a spinning motor is also a generator, so it pushes back against the battery. The proper name is back EMF. Stopped, nothing pushes back, so lots of current flows. As speed climbs, push-back grows and current falls. Think of starting a bike in a high gear: a huge shove at first, then less and less as you get moving. If a kid asks: yes, that loop is real feedback, built into the physics.
