Deck 8 · Understanding motors

6

This is the mental model drawn as a chart. Each line is the same CIM at a different voltage, so moving the stick slides the whole line. The flat dashed line is an example load we picked, 0.6 newton metres. A motor settles where its line crosses the load. Look along the dashed line: same torque, same current, about 33 amps, at three different speeds. Voltage changed the speed. The load decided the current. That is why a robot pushing a wall pulls huge current while going nowhere. In control theory this is the plant: what the motor does with the voltage you send it.
