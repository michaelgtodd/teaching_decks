Deck 8 · Understanding motors

5

(1.5 min) Power is how fast a motor does work. It is torque times speed, in radians per second. At stall you have torque but no speed, so zero. At free speed you have speed but no torque, also zero. Torque falls as speed rises, so the biggest product is in the middle: half torque times half speed. For a CIM that is 336 watts at 2655 RPM. At three quarters speed you get three quarters of the speed but one quarter of the torque, 252 watts. Peak power is not a place to sit, as we will see when we look at heat.
