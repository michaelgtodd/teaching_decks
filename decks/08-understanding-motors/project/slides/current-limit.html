Deck 8 · Understanding motors

12

Simulated, not measured. The model is four CIMs using the numbers in WPILib DCMotor.getCIM, 8.45:1 gearing, 6 inch wheels and an assumed 50 kg robot, with no friction, wheel slip or battery sag. The limit is an ideal clamp. The SPARK turns its output down until motor current holds. The cost is acceleration, because less current means less torque, but top speed stays. With battery sag added, this launch stays at about 10.1 volts on the fresh battery, not 6.5. A worn battery still falls to 4.8, so a limit doesn’t replace a charged battery. FIRST uses 60 A to reduce breaker trips and CIM damage. A setting is a request, so check your own log.
