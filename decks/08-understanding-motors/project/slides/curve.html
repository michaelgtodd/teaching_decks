(2 min) This is a motor curve, for the CIM on your KitBot. Speed runs along the bottom. Each line is a percent of its own biggest value, because they have different units. Now find four numbers. Left edge: stall, the motor is stuck, 2.42 newton metres and 133 amps. Right edge: free speed, 5310 RPM, and 2.7 amps. Those come from WPILib’s DCMotor class, and the lines are drawn straight between them, so this is a model and not a test result. If you find a chart with different axes, look for the same four numbers at the ends of its lines.
