Checking your math with DCMotor

MotorCheck.java

public static void print() {

  // withReduction() applies the gearing to the motor's numbers.

  DCMotor side = DCMotor.getCIM(2).withReduction(GEARING);

  DCMotor cim = DCMotor.getCIM(1);

 

  double topSpeed = side.freeSpeedRadPerSec * WHEEL_RADIUS_METERS;

  double oneStalled = cim.getCurrent(0, NOMINAL_VOLTS);

 

  System.out.printf("Top speed, free-speed math: %.2f m/s%n", topSpeed);

  System.out.printf("Stall current: %.0f A per CIM, %.0f A for four%n",

      oneStalled, DRIVE_MOTORS * oneStalled);

}

withReduction() gears the motor down, so freeSpeedRadPerSec is now wheel speed. Radians per second × radius = metres per second.

(1 min) WPILib already knows these motors. getCIM(2) means two CIMs in one gearbox, which is one side of the KitBot. withReduction applies your ratio, and hands back a new DCMotor with the speed divided and the torque multiplied, the two steps you did by hand. getCurrent with speed zero and 12 volts asks the stall question. You can swap in getNEO or getKrakenX60 and compare motors without touching a robot. The next slide is the exercise. Do the paper part before you run this.
