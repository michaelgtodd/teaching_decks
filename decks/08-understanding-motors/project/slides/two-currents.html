Simulated, not measured: the model from the next slide. A SPARK trades volts for amps. At a stop it switches on 45% of the time, so the motor sees 5.4 volts, and 60 amps in the motor is only 27 amps from the battery. Both are about 325 watts. REV’s rule: battery current equals motor current times duty cycle. REV calls motor current phase current. It stress-tested NEOs up to 80 amp limits behind a 40 amp breaker, and the breaker never tripped. Talon FX calls these stator and supply current.
