Best practice

A limit for every motor

Give every motor its own limit, chosen for its job.

REV suggests 40 to 60 A for a NEO and 20 to 40 A for a NEO 550. FIRST uses 60 A on KitBot CIMs. In REV’s stall test a NEO 550 lasted about 27 s at 40 A, and about 2 s at 80 A, the SPARK default.

This goes for every motor on the robot, not just the drivetrain. The 80 amp default suits some motors, but not a small one like a NEO 550. REV’s test held the motor stalled at each limit, and warns those times are not guaranteed, so leave a wide margin. A mechanism that can jam, like an intake or an arm against a hard stop, needs the most care. You can raise a limit later if your logs show the mechanism needs it.
