These curves are simulated, not measured. The model is an arm with no gravity: 15 degrees per second per volt, a 0.4 second lag, a 12 volt limit and a 20 ms control loop, using only P to reach 90 degrees. A control loop measures, compares and corrects. When you tune, you are part of a loop too. PIDController is Sendable, so Elastic shows boxes for kP, kI, kD and Setpoint. Click Publish Values to send them. Enter does not. Put a Graph of the angle beside it. Deck 6 goes deeper. Timing: two minutes.
