Live tuning: Enter disables an enabled robot

What you see

You type a new value into a box, press Enter, and the enabled robot stops.

Why it happens

Enter is the Driver Station shortcut for Disable, even while you are typing.

The fix

Robot enabled? Click the box’s submit button: Show Submit Button in Edit Properties.
