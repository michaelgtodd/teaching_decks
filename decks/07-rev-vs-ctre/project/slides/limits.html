Two terms. Stator current is the current inside the motor, and it makes torque and heat. Supply current is what comes out of the battery. SPARK Smart Current Limit holds phase current, which is the motor side. A soft limit is a position the controller refuses to drive past, so your code cannot push an arm through the frame. It only works if your zero is right. SPARK also has a blunt current chop as a backstop. Brownout is the roboRIO protecting itself, so if you see it often, check the battery and wiring first.
