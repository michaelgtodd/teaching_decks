Cost

Free features and paid features

Without Pro, a request with EnableFOC falls back to normal drive, but TorqueCurrentFOC will not run. A Season Pass covers 100 devices for one team number. Pro features are free in simulation.

REV: one tier

REVLib, Hardware Client 2, StatusLogger, MAXMotion and simulation. REV’s docs list no paid software.

Phoenix 6: free

Volt and duty cycle closed loops. Motion Magic, Velocity and Expo. Swerve API. Tuner X. Simulation. Limited log export.

CANivore: hardware

A second CAN bus for CTRE devices, with CAN FD and CANivore timestamps.

Pro license

FOC and torque control on Talon FX. FOC gives about 15% more peak power. FusedCANcoder, SyncCANcoder, full log export, hoot replay.

Pro and CANivore

Timesync, device timestamps, Dynamic Motion Magic, full differential control.

Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

10

FOC, field oriented control, times the motor’s magnetic field more cleverly, for about 15 percent more peak power. Talon FXS does not support it. A CANivore is a USB box that adds a second, faster CAN bus, and it costs money but needs no license. Pro is optional. Fused CANcoder blends the absolute sensor with the motor’s fast sensor. A license matches the firmware year, so check before you rely on an old one.
