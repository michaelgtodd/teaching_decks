Exercise

Pick the parts for one mechanism

4 min

1.

Choose one mechanism: an arm, a shooter or an intake.

2.

Pick a motor and a controller. Check them on the pairing table.

3.

Write down its current limit and where its sensor plugs in.

4.

Open RevArm.java or CtreArm.java and find the gravity lines.

Done when: your group can name the motor, controller, current limit and sensor, and point to the gravity lines.

Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

16

Work in pairs. Mix programmers with electrical students if you can, since the sensor question is partly theirs. Either vendor can be a correct answer. Walk around and ask “why that one?” If a group finishes early, have them work out the same pick with the other vendor and list what changes. Back in four minutes. Timing: 4 minutes.
