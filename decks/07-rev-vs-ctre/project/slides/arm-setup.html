    .apply(AbsoluteEncoderConfig.Presets

        .REV_ThroughBoreEncoderV2)

    .zeroOffset(ENCODER_OFFSET_ROT);

        FeedbackSensor.kAbsoluteEncoder)

feedback.FeedbackSensorSource =

    FeedbackSensorSourceValue.RemoteCANcoder;

Here is the same arm, with an absolute encoder on the arm shaft. On the left, a Through Bore Encoder is wired to the SPARK. On the right, a CANcoder talks over CAN, free as RemoteCANcoder. Its readings arrive over the bus, and Pro’s FusedCANcoder blends in the fast motor sensor. Highlighted lines are gravity. kCosRatio is 1 because the encoder turns once per arm rotation. We set no conversion factors, because SPARK firmware 27 removes them. Safety: SPARK MAX 27.0.0 lists a fix for closed loop using the wrong sensor after a reboot, in a setup like this one. On 26.1.5, test a reboot before you trust it. Gains are starting guesses.
