5

Think of a shower. If hot water takes ages to arrive, you over-turn the tap. Delay makes a controller overshoot. This is a simulation, not a robot: a 2 kg arm, 0.5 m long, 60:1 on a NEO 2.0, a 40 A limit, kG feedforward and P only, nudged from 45 to 55 degrees. The 1 kHz loop sees a 1 ms old angle. We assume 50 Hz sees a 20 ms old angle. Same gain is 24 volts per radian. Lower gain is 10.5, set to overshoot no more than the 1 kHz curve. On a full 45 degree move the 12 V limit matters more, so the gap shrinks to 0.23 s against 0.31 s. Timing: 3 minutes.
