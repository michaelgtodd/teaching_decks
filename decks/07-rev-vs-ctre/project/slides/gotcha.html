Common mistake

Newest firmware is not always the right firmware

What you see

A SPARK shows “Outdated REV Device”, REVLib says the firmware is too old or too new, or Phoenix reports a firmware version error.

Why it happens

On both vendors, firmware and library are a matched pair for each season. The newest firmware listed can be for next season.

The fix

For 2026, pick the newest 26.x in Hardware Client 2, and the 2026 firmware year in Tuner X.

Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

Both vendors work this way. Hardware Client 2 needs 26.x or newer, so a SPARK on 2025 firmware goes through Recovery Mode once. There, the version menu automatically picks the most up-to-date firmware, so stop and choose yours. On CTRE, the status code says firmware and API major versions must match, and Tuner X has a year selector. Its changelog already lists 26.70 firmware, for a 2027 alpha API that does not support the roboRIO. New Talon FX built after April 2026 need 26.3.0.0 or newer. Timing: 1 minute.
