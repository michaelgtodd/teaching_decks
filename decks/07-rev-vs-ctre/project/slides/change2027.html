Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

17

One item is already real: REV released SPARK firmware 27.0.0 on October 29. Positions are in native sensor units, and velocity is in units per minute. That is why our arm uses rotations and converts in Java, so it does not depend on conversion factors. Everything marked alpha is for Systemcore, the controller replacing the roboRIO, and may change. Systemcore has more CAN buses, so every device must say which bus it is on, which is why we used CANBus today. If you search REV’s online Java docs, you may land on this alpha API, so trust your installed library. Timing: 1 minute.
