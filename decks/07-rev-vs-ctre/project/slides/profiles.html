Motion profiles on the controller

MAXMotion (REV)

Position: a trapezoid. Speed up, cruise, slow down.

You set cruise velocity, max acceleration and allowed profile error.

Drift too far off the plan and the SPARK makes a new plan from where it is.

Velocity: ramps to a speed at your max acceleration.

Motion Magic (CTRE)

Motion Magic: a trapezoid, or a smoother S-curve if you set jerk.

Velocity: ramps to a speed at your acceleration, with optional jerk.

Expo: an exponential curve from kV and kA that fits what the motor can do.

Dynamic: change the limits in every request. Needs Pro and a CANivore.

Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

7

A motion profile is a plan for the whole move, not just the end point. Without one, PID sees a big error and slams the motor. With one, the controller feeds PID a target that moves smoothly. Plot speed against time and you get a trapezoid: up, flat, down. Jerk is how fast acceleration changes, and limiting it rounds the corners. All of this runs on the controller, so you send one target and it does the rest. MAXMotion updates its profile every 10 ms and its PID every 1 ms. MAXMotion velocity ignores cruise velocity.
