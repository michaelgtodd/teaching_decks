Who can help

Mixing is allowed

R504 lists both vendors and one project can use both libraries. You pay with two apps and two update routines.

Deck 7 · REV vs CTRE

15

There is no winner here. REV gives you everything with no paid tier, separate parts, and encoders that wire straight to the controller. CTRE gives you built-in controllers, code generators and deep logging, and some features cost extra. Check today’s store pages for prices, because they change. Mixing is legal and practical: a Talon FXS can even drive a NEO. Just know that following stays within a vendor, SPARKs stay on the roboRIO CAN bus, and Phoenix swerve needs all CTRE. The best motor is the one your team can keep running. Timing: 2 minutes.
