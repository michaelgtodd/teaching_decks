Control theory

Three gains predict the volts a drivetrain needs

Volts = kS + kV × speed + kA × acceleration. Deck 6 did this for an arm.

kS: the first shove

A heavy box won’t slide until you push hard enough. We assume 1 V of friction.

kV: the steady push

A spinning motor pushes back, so speed costs volts: 2.34 V per m/s.

kA: the extra push

A loaded cart fights you while it gains speed: 0.56 V per m/s².
