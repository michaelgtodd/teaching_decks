Deck 6’s trapezoid is speed against time, and its corners are moments when the push changes all at once. These are the planned speeds for Deck 6’s arm, computed, with a jerk we picked for the example: 30 rad/s cubed. The S-curve covers the same distance and takes longer. Chains and tall arms benefit from rounded corners. Start with a trapezoid, and reach for an S-curve only if a mechanism shakes at the corners.
