(1 min) Picture the free play in a bike’s brake lever: you move it a little and nothing happens yet. A deadband builds that in on purpose. A gamepad stick rarely rests at exactly zero, and without a deadband a resting stick can creep the robot. These lines are exact math, the same formula as MathUtil.applyDeadband. Look at the orange jump: a plain if statement goes from nothing to 8 percent in one step. applyDeadband starts again from zero, so the first bit of real movement is gentle.
