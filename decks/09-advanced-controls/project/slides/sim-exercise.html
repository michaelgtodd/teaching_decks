Exercise

Tune the speed loop in the simulator

1.

In Constants.java, set KS, KV and KA to 0. Run Simulate Robot Code.

2.

Enable and hold A: the goal is 2 m/s. Note where Drive/Left speed (mps) stops.

3.

Put back KV, then KS, then KA. Hold A after each one.

4.

Hold A and Y. Y is a simulated push. Raise KP until the push barely matters.

Done when: with A and Y held, Drive/Left speed (mps) stays within 0.05 of 2 m/s.
