A slew-rate limiter limits how fast a number can change. Top speed stays the same and takes longer to reach. This plot is simulated, not measured: four CIMs from WPILib DCMotor.getCIM, 8.45:1 gearing, 6 inch wheels and an assumed 50 kg robot, with no friction, wheel slip or battery sag. The limiter updates every 20 ms. While the limiter catches up, the robot keeps moving after the stick is released. The SPARK has openLoopRampRate too, but it can’t tell forward from turning.
