Deck 9 · Advanced controls

9

(1.5 min) Think of a shower: you know roughly where the tap goes, then you adjust by feel. Feedforward is knowing, and P is feeling. The goal ramps to 2 m/s, then an assumed 80 newton push arrives, like a defender leaning on you. P alone has to stay wrong to make any volts, so it stops short. Feedforward alone lands on the goal but cannot see the push. Together you get both. This is simulated, with an assumed 1 volt of friction, and our real Java landed within 0.01 m/s of the model in the WPILib simulator.
