(1.5 min) Think of a check-up at the doctor: a few set tests, and out come your numbers. This plot is simulated: the model runs SysId’s two tests, then the same kind of fit SysId does. The routine runs four tests, slow and fast, forward and back, and logs them. The SysId app reads the log and gives you the gains. Our real SysIdRoutine, on the simulated robot, found 1.03, 2.34 and 0.56. The slow test here used 4.8 m, so use the floor, not blocks: WPILib says a drive needs 10 to 20 feet. Hold the button to run, so letting go stops it. Treat suggested P and D gains as a starting point.
