Common mistake

The wrong 2026 field layout

What you see

Vision poses land away from where the robot really is, even with a good calibration.

Why it happens

REBUILT has two field layouts, welded and AndyMark. WPILib’s default and PhotonVision ship with welded.

The fix

Use the layout for the field you are on, in your code and on the coprocessor.

AprilTagFieldLayout.loadField(

    AprilTagFields.k2026RebuiltAndymark);

PhotonVision’s multi-tag page warns that differences between the layout and the real tag positions cause error in the pose. In WPILib 2026, AprilTagFields has k2026RebuiltWelded and k2026RebuiltAndymark, and the default field is welded. The layout matters in two places: the PhotonPoseEstimator in your code, and the field layout on the PhotonVision coprocessor for multi-tag. Limelight uses the field map you upload. Find out which field your event uses before you arrive.
