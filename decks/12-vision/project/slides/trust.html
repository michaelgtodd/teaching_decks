6

This is computed from WPILib’s formula, not simulated. A standard deviation says how spread out a measurement’s noise is: bigger means trust it less. The estimator starts 1 meter off, the robot stands still, and a vision pose arrives every 20 ms. Each pose moves the estimate by K of the gap. K depends on both numbers: WPILib’s default odometry value is 0.02 m, and you give the vision value with each pose. 0.5 and 4 are the multi-tag and one-tag values in PhotonVision’s example. Small numbers snap the estimate to vision, noise included. Big ones barely move it.
