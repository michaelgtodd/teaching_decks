This deck builds on Deck 10. There, odometry estimated the robot’s pose from encoders and a gyro. A pose is a position on the field plus the direction the robot faces. Odometry drifts, because wheels slip. Vision corrects it. The deck then compares the products, counts what 2026 team repositories contain, and ends with a recommendation and an exercise in the simulator.
