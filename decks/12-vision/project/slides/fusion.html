WPILib docs: Pose Estimators

5

This is WPILib’s pose estimator, from its source. Odometry runs every loop and the estimator keeps the last 1.5 seconds of poses. A camera frame arrives late: it took time to capture, process and send. So the estimator looks up where it thought the robot was at the moment the frame was taken, measures the gap to the vision pose, and moves only part of the way. Then it replays the odometry since that moment. This is why every vision pose comes with a timestamp. A measurement older than 1.5 seconds is ignored.
