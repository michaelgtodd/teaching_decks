WPILib docs: What Are AprilTags?

3

AprilTags were made at the University of Michigan for robot localization. Since 2024, FIRST uses the 36h11 family: a 6 by 6 grid of bits inside a border. The printed tag body is 6.5 inches. WPILib’s AprilTagFieldLayout class holds the pose of every tag on the field. A camera finds a tag, decodes its ID, and measures its four corners. With a calibrated camera, the corners give the camera’s pose relative to the tag. Add the tag’s known place on the field and where the camera sits on the robot, and you get the robot’s pose on the field.
