6 min

1.

Copy periodic() from Level 1 into your CANDriveSubsystem, add the imports, and deploy.

2.

Put the robot on blocks, wheels off the floor. No robot? getAppliedOutput() reads 0 in the simulator, so run Level 3 and graph Left Motor Speed under Robot/container/drive/drive.

3.

Connect AdvantageScope, then open SmartDashboard and Drive in the sidebar.

4.

Drag LeftAppliedOutput and RightAppliedOutput to Left Axis.

5.

Enable. Push the left stick forward, then use the right stick to turn.

Done when: both lines follow the sticks, and they move differently when you turn.

Ask for a guess first: what will full stick read? Most people say 1. It reads about 0.5, because DRIVE_SCALE is 0.7 and arcadeDrive squares its inputs by default, and 0.7 squared is 0.49. That is a real finding from a graph. One side is inverted in the config (the left, on the KitBot), so one line may mirror the other. If so, have them work out why. Teams with no robot free can pair up, or use the simulator: there, getAppliedOutput reads 0 because nothing is simulating the SPARK, so those two lines stay flat and that is not a bug. With the Level 3 Epilogue code, Left Motor Speed and Right Motor Speed carry real values in the simulator. Safety first: wheels off the floor, and someone on the Driver Station ready to disable.
