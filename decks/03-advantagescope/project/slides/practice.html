What to log and how to name it

Log inputs and outputs, put units in the names, and log more than you think you need.

You cannot record something after the match is over. Inputs are what the robot sensed. Outputs are what it asked for. With both, you can tell whether the code or the machine went wrong.

Here is why inputs and outputs both matter. If the stick said full forward and the motor output was zero, look at the code. If the output was full and nothing moved, look at the robot. A number with no unit is a guess, and the unit has to be the last word in the name for AdvantageScope to read it. Log generously, because disk space is cheap and a repeat of the problem is not. If you see loop overruns, trim the slow reads. (1 minute)
