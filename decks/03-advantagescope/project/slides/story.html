This example is made up: a team raises DRIVE_SCALE to 1 and the robot stutters late in practice. Below 6.3 V a roboRIO 1 cuts the motors, then they return above 7.5 V. A roboRIO 2 defaults to 6.75. Simulated: 4 CIMs, 60 A limit, 50 kg, 8.45 to 1, 6 inch wheels, checked every 20 ms, motors coast when cut. Assumed batteries, including wiring: 12.3 V with 0.035 ohms, 12.9 V with 0.022. In AdvantageScope, put voltage on Left Axis, current on Right Axis, and BrownedOut on Discrete Fields. The Driver Station log has voltage too, with no code.
