Getting connected

Starting AdvantageScope and connecting to a robot

1

Open it

In WPILib VS Code: Ctrl+Shift+P, then WPILib: Start Tool, then AdvantageScope.

2

Set your robot address once

App > Show Preferences... > Robot Address. Team 1212 is 10.12.12.2.

3

Connect

File > Connect to Robot > Default: NetworkTables 4, or press Ctrl+K.

4

Watch the window title

It says Searching until the robot answers.

5

On a USB cable?

File > Use USB roboRIO Address switches to 172.22.11.2.

Do this live on the projector. The address pattern is 10.TE.AM.2, where TE.AM is your team number split up, so team 1212 is 10.12.12.2. On a Mac the settings are under AdvantageScope, then Settings.
