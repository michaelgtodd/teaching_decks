Logs

Getting a log off the robot

1

Best: a USB stick in the roboRIO

FAT32, in place before code starts. Logs go in a logs folder on the stick.

2

No stick?

Logs go to /home/lvuser/logs. WPILib warns against this on a roboRIO 1.

3

Copy it

Pull the stick with the robot off, or use File > Download Logs....

4

Check the folder

Robot Log Path starts as /U/logs, the stick. Change it for internal storage.

5

Open it

File > Open Log(s).... REV .revlog and CTRE .hoot files open the same way.

9

In the download window, pick your logs and click the down arrow. REV StatusLogger is on by default in 2026 and AdvantageScope converts its .revlog files, though they cannot be precisely synced with other logs. CTRE Signal Logger writes .hoot files. AdvantageScope asks you to accept the CTRE license first, and logs without Pro devices show fewer signals. The Driver Station can also copy logs: click the gear, then Upload WPILogs.
