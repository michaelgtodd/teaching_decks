The setpoint is where you asked to go. The measurement is where it really went. Every tuning job comes down to comparing those two lines. Think of a shower: you turn the tap, it goes too hot, then settles a bit too cool. Those four numbers name what you saw. Simulated: a second-order model with natural frequency 6 rad/s, damping ratio 0.4, and a constant load, logged every 20 ms. It is not a real arm. Rise and settling are measured against the final value, and textbooks often measure overshoot that way too. Deck 6 tunes these numbers. (3 minutes)
