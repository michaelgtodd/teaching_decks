Every subsystem can have simulationPeriodic. The command scheduler calls it every loop, but only in simulation, so on the roboRIO this never runs. We turn output into volts: Deck 2 turned on voltage compensation at 12 volts, so 0.5 means 6 volts. On a real robot, disabling stops the motors. Our saved numbers don’t know that, and Motor Safety does nothing while disabled. Without this if statement, the sim robot keeps driving after you disable it. Test that yourself later.
