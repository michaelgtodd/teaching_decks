This is the model, and it’s short. createKitbotSim builds a physics model of the kit drivetrain. We tell it two CIMs per side, 8.45 to 1 gearing and six inch wheels, the standard setup in AndyMark’s AM14U6 guide. The null means no fake sensor noise. StructPublisher sends the pose the way AdvantageScope’s docs recommend. step does the work: feed in volts, move time forward, publish. If a kid asks about Field2d: it works too, but struct data is what AdvantageScope asks for first.
