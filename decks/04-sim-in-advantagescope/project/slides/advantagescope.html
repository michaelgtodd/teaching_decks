Open AdvantageScope

2

Connect to the simulator

File > Connect to Simulator > Default, or Ctrl+Shift+K.

3

Add a 2D Field tab

Use the plus button in the tab bar.

4

Pick the 2026 field

Use the Field list in the panel under the view.

5

Drag SimPose onto Poses

Deck 4 · Sim in AdvantageScope

2027: field origin moves to the center of the field

10

Keep the simulator running. Start AdvantageScope. File, Connect to Simulator, Default. You don’t need an address, because it looks on your own computer. Open a 2D Field tab, pick the 2026 field, and drag SimPose from the sidebar onto Poses. Use the search box if you can’t see it. If 2026 isn’t in the list: season fields download in the background when AdvantageScope is online, and with no internet you can use Evergreen. Now put the windows side by side and click the Sim GUI before you drive.
