Control theory calls the thing you’re controlling the plant. A model is math that predicts it. Follow the chain. Volts go into the motor and become torque. Gearing multiplies torque by 8.45 and cuts speed by the same amount. Force on the robot’s mass gives acceleration, and speed adds up into a pose. Now the loop at the bottom: a spinning motor acts like a generator and pushes back, which is called back-EMF. Faster motor, less push. The whole chain steps every 20 milliseconds.
