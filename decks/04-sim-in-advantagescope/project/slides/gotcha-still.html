Common mistake

The robot ignores you

What you see

You press keys or move the stick, and the robot sits still.

Why it happens

Robot State isn’t on Teleoperated, Joystick[0] is empty, or another window has keyboard focus.

The fix

Click Teleoperated. Drag your device onto Joystick[0]. Click the Sim GUI window before you use the keys.
