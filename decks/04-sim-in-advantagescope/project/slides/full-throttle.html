This was computed, not drawn: WPILib’s drivetrain equation with DCMotor.getCIM(2) constants, 8.45 to 1, six inch wheels and 12 volts held steady, stepped every 20 ms. Speed rises fast, then flattens as back-EMF grows. You’ll see that shape all over control theory. The model tops out at 16.8 feet per second. AndyMark’s 12.7 assumes CIMs at 4100 rpm, about 75 percent of free speed. The first step asks for about 4 g, which real tires and our 60 amp limit wouldn’t allow. createKitbotSim assumes 60 pounds; 120 is a what-if.
