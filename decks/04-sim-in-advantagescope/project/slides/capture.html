Deck 2 gave DifferentialDrive two motors. It has a second constructor that takes two methods, and the motor version just calls this one, so the robot behaves the same. Each method saves the output, then sends it on. Why not ask the SPARK? REV’s docs say applied output needs extra sim code, and they don’t cover brushed mode, which is what CIMs use. Saving it ourselves is simple and needs nothing from REV. Bonus: forward is positive on both sides, because the SPARK flips one side later.
