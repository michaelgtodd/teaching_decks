Exercise

Drive a figure-8, then change the robot

8 min

1.

Drive a figure-8. Then time one full-stick trip across the field.

2.

In DriveSim, change k8p45 to k5p95 or k12p75. Time the same trip.

3.

Change it back. In RobotContainer, pass this::slewedForward to driveArcade.

4.

Slam the stick from stop to full. Watch how the start changes.

Done when: you have two trip times and can say what the limiter did.

Deck 4 · Sim in AdvantageScope

17

Eight minutes, and mentors walk the room. Deck 2 scaled the stick to 0.7 and arcadeDrive squares that, so full stick is about 0.49, or about 8 feet per second. Time the same trip before changing anything. Gearing changes top speed: 5.95 is faster, 12.75 is slower. Then try the slew rate limiter, which limits how fast a number can change, so it softens a hard start. Done early? Ask what they’d expect on a real robot, and why.
