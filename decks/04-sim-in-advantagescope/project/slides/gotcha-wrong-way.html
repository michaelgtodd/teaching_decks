Common mistake

The robot drives the wrong way

What you see

You push forward and the robot backs up, spins, or crosses the screen the wrong way.

Why it happens

Backs up: a stick isn’t negated. Spins: one side has a flipped sign. Wrong way on screen: check the view first, because heading 0 faces the red wall.

The fix

Negate both sticks in RobotContainer. Pass both saved outputs to setInputs with no minus signs.

Three problems look like wrong way. Backing up means the stick sign is missing: pushing away reads negative, so Deck 2 negates it. Spinning means one side has the wrong sign. WPILib’s docs say to negate an inverted right side, but we saved the output before the SPARK inverts it, so both go in unchanged. Last, check the view: AdvantageScope can rotate the field. A stick mistake you find here is one you won’t meet on the real robot.
