Image the roboRIO

Image a roboRIO 1 with the Imaging Tool

1

Check which roboRIO you have

A microSD card slot means roboRIO 2: use the next slide instead.

2

Connect the USB cable

Printer-style A to B cable, in the USB Device port. Never image over Ethernet.

3

Open the roboRIO Imaging Tool

It scans for your roboRIO. Nothing found? Try Run as Administrator.

4

Click Format Target

Enter your team number, select the latest image, click Reformat.

5

Wait 3 to 10 minutes

Click OK, then Close.

6

Press the Reset button

The new team number takes effect after the reboot.

8

One and a half minutes. The image and the firmware are different things. The image is the operating system, and you must update it every year. The firmware is only used in safe mode, and only a brand-new roboRIO needs it updated: version 5.0 or higher is fine. Check that the roboRIO power connector is secure before you start, all four screws. If imaging fails, try a different USB cable, turn off the firewall, or try another laptop. The official page lists more fixes. A roboRIO 2 needs the next slide.
