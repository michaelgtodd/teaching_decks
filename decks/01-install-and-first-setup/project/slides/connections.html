Two minutes. Trace it with your finger. Your laptop talks by Ethernet to a radio on a wall, the access point. That talks by 6 GHz Wi-Fi to the radio on the robot, which plugs into the roboRIO. The roboRIO sends commands over CAN along a chain of motor controllers, ending at the Power Distribution Hub, or PDH, which needs its termination on. At events, the field’s access point replaces yours. The electrical workshop covers wiring in depth, so we stop here. If a kid asks about the arrows: data flows both ways.
