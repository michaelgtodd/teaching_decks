Two minutes. Driving a car works like this. Your eyes see the road, you decide, your hands move the wheel, the car moves, and you look again. Your robot program does the same, and TimedRobot runs it every 20 milliseconds by default. Because each loop sees the result of the last one, this is a feedback loop, and deck 6 builds on it. Never wait inside a periodic method. A long loop gives the warning, Loop time of 0.02s overrun. If a kid asks what happens then: the robot reacts late.
