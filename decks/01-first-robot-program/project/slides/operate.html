WPILib docs: Running your Test Program

22

This follows WPILib’s sequence in Running your Test Program: confirm the battery voltage, confirm the three lights are green, choose Teleop, enable, then disable. Communications means the laptop can reach the roboRIO. Robot Code means the program is running. Joysticks means a controller is plugged in, and F1 makes Driver Station look for controllers again. Space works even if another window has focus. After an emergency stop the roboRIO must reboot before the robot can be enabled again, so use Enter for ordinary stops.
