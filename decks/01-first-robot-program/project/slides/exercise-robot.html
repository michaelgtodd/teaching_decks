Exercise

Spin the motor

1.

Deploy the program, open Driver Station and get three green lights.

2.

Stand clear of the motor and anything it drives.

3.

Choose TeleOperated, say “enabling” out loud, then click Enable.

4.

Hold A, then release it.

5.

Press Enter to disable.

Done when: the motor spins only while A is held.

Deck 1 · Your first robot program

23

Saying “enabling” warns everyone nearby. Disabling also cancels the command, which sets the output to 0. A negative kSpeed spins the motor the other way. If nothing moves, check that Driver Station shows Teleoperated Enabled and that the controller is in slot 0 of the USB tab. An error naming a CAN ID means no SPARK MAX answered at that ID, and Deck 2 covers that error.
