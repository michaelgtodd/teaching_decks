Your turn

Turn the robot on safely

15 min

1.

A mentor checks: wheels off the ground, hands clear.

2.

One person sits at the laptop. Everyone else stands back and watches the light.

3.

Choose Teleoperated, say “enabling” out loud, then click Enable.

4.

Watch the robot signal light blink, then press Enter.

5.

Swap, so everyone gets a turn.

Done when: The light blinks when enabled and goes solid after Enter. Nothing spins today, and that is correct.

Deck 1 · Your first robot program

17

Before anyone enables, point to the robot signal light. Then say this: nothing will spin today, and that is correct. This practice code is not set up for our motors. The win is the blinking light, because it means your robot is listening to your laptop. Teleoperated means driver control. Saying enabling out loud warns everyone nearby. Use this checklist every time, even when nothing should move, because old code may still be on the robot. Timing: 15 minutes.
