These are the same parts you just found, plus your laptop. Trace it with your finger, left to right. You ask for something on the laptop. The radio carries that message to the robot. The roboRIO runs your code, which decides what to do. The motor controller sets the power, and the motor turns the wheel. Today we use a USB cable in place of the radio, because a cable is simpler to get working. Timing: 3 minutes.
