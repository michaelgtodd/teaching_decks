95 min

Welcome, everyone. We have one goal: by the end, code you made is running on a real robot. There are two parts, with a break between them. In part one you meet the robot and get your laptop ready. In part two your group sends code to the robot and turns it on safely. Mentors: part one takes about 45 minutes and part two about 50, so do not run them back to back. Timing: 1 minute.
