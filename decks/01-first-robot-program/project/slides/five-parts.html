Get everyone around the robot with the power off. Read one card at a time, and have someone point to that part before you move on. Touching is fine while it is off. Our motor controllers are called SPARK MAX, and you will hear that name a lot. Do not explain wiring here, because the electrical workshop covers it. If a kid asks what a word means, that is a great question, so stop and answer it. Timing: 6 minutes.
