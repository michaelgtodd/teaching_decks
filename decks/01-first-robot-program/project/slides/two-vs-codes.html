Common mistake

The WPILib commands are missing

What you see

You press Ctrl+Shift+P, type WPILib, and nothing shows up.

Why it happens

You opened your everyday VS Code. WPILib comes with its own separate copy.

The fix

Close it and open the one with 2026 in its name.

This one catches a lot of people, so it is not your fault. If you already had VS Code on your laptop, you now have two copies. They look almost the same. Only the 2026 copy has the WPILib tools. Ctrl+Shift+P opens a search box for commands, and we will use it all day. Timing: 1 minute.
