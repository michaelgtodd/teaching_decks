Deck 6 gave you a loop: setpoint, measurement, error. A path follower is the same loop, except the setpoint moves. The trajectory is the plan: at 1.2 seconds, be here, going this fast. Feedforward sends the speed the plan already asks for. Feedback looks at the gap between the plan and where odometry says you are, and adds a small correction. Then each wheel side gets its own smaller loop, as in Deck 6. All of it runs every 20 milliseconds. As in Deck 6, feedforward does the work and PID cleans up. The wheel loop runs inside the pose loop.
