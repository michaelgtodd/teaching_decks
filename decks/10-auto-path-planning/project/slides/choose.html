(1.5 min) Here is our advice. Stock KitBot: stay left. Once you add sensors, tune the drivetrain, then check odometry with a tape measure: roll the robot 3 meters and read the pose. Then use PathPlanner. Most teams use it, so help is easy to find, it works on tank and swerve, and the follower is included. Add Choreo only when your autos work and path speed is what is holding you back. PathPlanner can load Choreo paths, and 28 repos in our sample had Choreo paths without ChoreoLib. A swerve team can try BLine, but it is not where I would start. WPILib alone works for learning.
