Common mistake

The path is perfect, in the wrong place

What you see

The curve looks right and the logs say the robot hit its target, but the real robot misses.

Why it happens

Resetting odometry tells the robot where it is, and it believes you. It can’t tell you placed it wrong.

The fix

Measure your starting pose, and line up the same way every match. In sim, 10° crooked ended 55 cm off.

(1 min) This one catches everybody, so do not feel bad when it happens. We tried it in the simulator. Starting the robot 10 degrees crooked, it ended 55 cm from the goal, while odometry reported it was within about 1 cm. Starting 50 cm too far forward, it ended 50 cm off in the same way. The robot followed the path perfectly, in its own mind. Odometry only measures change from where you said it started. So your starting spot is part of your code. Choose something on the field you can line up against, and practice it.
