Drive to Hub starts at x 1.6 m, y 6.4 m, heading minus 45 degrees, and ends at 3.3 by 4.7, heading minus 27 degrees, which points at the hub at about 4.6 by 4.0. The field origin is the corner of the blue alliance wall, and you always draw on the blue side. The code mirrors paths for red. Exact numbers can be typed in the Waypoints tree. A tank drive faces the way it travels, so a heading also sets which way the robot points. The outline drawn on the path is the robot, from the bumper settings.
