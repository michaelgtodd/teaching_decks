Deck 10’s CANDriveSubsystem has all four. This deck reuses it. A stock KitBot has no encoders or gyro.

Deck 11 · PathPlanner

4

A path follower compares where the robot should be with where it is, so it needs to measure position. That takes wheel encoders and a gyro, combined by odometry. Deck 10 covered this, and its drivetrain adds those sensors to the KitBot and passes the four methods to PathPlannerLib. Robot-relative speeds mean forward and turning speeds as the robot sees them, not along the field. Without sensors, start with the timed autonomous from Deck 2.
