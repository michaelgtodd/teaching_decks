The 6 GHz key is the password the two radios share, so write it down. The robot radio goes on the robot. The access point plugs into your laptop, and it stands in for the field at home. Using a battery on the access point is not legal, and the radio turns its wireless off. At official events, radios are programmed at a table called the Radio Kiosk, so you do not need this page there.
