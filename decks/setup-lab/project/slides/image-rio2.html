Job 3: image the roboRIO

Image a roboRIO 2

1

Find the image file

In the Imaging Tool, click the SD button. Use the newest FRC_roboRIO2 zip.

2

Put the card in a USB reader on your laptop

A card of 2 GB or more is recommended.

3

Write it with balenaEtcher or Raspberry Pi Imager

If Windows offers to format the card, say no.

4

Power off, insert the card, power on

5

Set the team number

Imaging Tool: Edit Startup Settings, Team Number, Apply.
